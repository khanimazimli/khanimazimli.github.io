About

An economist who ended up working on customers, and then on the systems around them.

I have spent ten years in customer-facing roles across four industries: retail, international education, shopping malls and, since 2025, banking. Each move took me further from producing communication and closer to deciding what an organisation should do differently.

Why consulting

What carries over

The work I enjoy most is the part before execution: taking an unclear business question, structuring it, finding the evidence, and leaving behind something other people can act on. That is the core of consulting work, and it is the thread through my career so far.

Structuring ambiguous problemsTurning “we should personalize more” into segments, rules, owners and a sequence of decisions.
Working across functionsCoordinating campaigns through Marketing, Data Science, Risk and Finance approval in a regulated organisation.
Evidence over opinionStarting from customer behaviour and research, and being explicit about what is measured, what is assumed and what is not known.
Building, not just recommendingUsing AI tools to turn a proposed process into a working prototype people can test.
