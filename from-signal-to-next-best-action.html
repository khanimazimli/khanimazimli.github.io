The problem · one customer, one day

Personalization was not the hard part. Coordination was.

Retail banking produces hundreds of signals a day, and each one creates an opportunity. Travel wants the trip. Deposits want the balance. Cards want usage back. Each campaign is relevant on its own. Together, they are noise.

      JRN-TRV-01 · v1.3 · Product adoption

      Travel card advantages

      Live in engine
      ENTRY 01
      What starts evaluation?

      Flight purchased, or first international card spend in 48 hours.

      ELIGIBILITY 02
      Who can enter?

      Active card holder, app user, marketing consent.

      EXCLUSIONS 03
      Who must not?

      Completed in last 90 days, open complaint, in retention recovery.

      PRIORITY 04
      When journeys collide?

      Yields to retention. Overrides seasonal and cashback.

      FREQUENCY 05
      How often?

      Max 2 touches, 1 push per 7 days, 3 CRM contacts per 7 days.

      CHANNEL 06
      Where?

      Story first. Push only within 3 days of departure. Banner as reminder.

      EXIT 07
      What completes it?

      First card transaction abroad, or 7 days after return.

      RE-ENTRY 08
      Can they return?

      After 60 days, and only with a new travel signal.

      MEASUREMENT 09
      What is success?

      Share of travellers using the card abroad, against a holdout group.

      Without these rules, journeys stay isolated campaigns. With them, CRM becomes an operating system.

      What this changed

      A model that scales.

      New journeys no longer launch as isolated campaigns. They enter the same decisioning architecture and compete by shared customer rules.
