02The problem

CRM knows the customer. It often ignores the moment.

Most CRM journeys start from what the bank already knows: transactions, balances, products, app activity, past campaigns. That describes the customer well. It says very little about the day they are having. This project added one signal from outside the bank, the weather, and tested whether it could become a real input into customer decisions.

04The decision

A signal alone is not enough.

Good weather for the offer does not mean every customer should hear about it. Each check removes people for whom a message would be irrelevant, repeated or badly timed. Only those who pass every check get the experience.

06The journey

The experience unfolds at the right moment.

In a scheduled campaign, someone decides Tuesday is campaign day. Here, nobody picks the date. The first qualifying day in the forecast becomes the key day, and the journey is planned backwards from it.
