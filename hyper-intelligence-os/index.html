Hyper Intelligence OS: from an operational problem to a working prototype.

I saw campaign preparation repeat the same work across six steps and several teams. I turned that into a product concept, and built a working prototype by orchestrating AI agents, with every decision kept human.

Problem
One campaign takes weeks across six stages owned by different teams, much of it restating the same facts.
My role
Initiated it on my own, own the product decisions, designed the workflow and built it with AI coding agents.
Why it was complex
Facts must stay identical from brief to copy, approvals in a bank must stay human, and image generation costs money.
What I built
Four modules that turn one sentence into a brief, a journey document, checked copy and a visual brief.
Demonstrated
Three modules working, one in testing on test campaigns. Not used on live campaigns.

01

From problem to product

  1. ProblemMapped where campaign preparation time goes

    Six stages owned by different teams, from brief to platform build. By my rough assumptions, the first four take most of the time and repeat the same facts in new formats; the decisions sit in review and approval.

    stage map with owners and rough duration assumptions; not measured
  2. RequirementsWrote three rules before building anything

    The brief step never invents a fact and asks for what is missing. Approvals stay human. Paid image generation needs explicit human consent.

    each rule is enforced in the prototype: rule-based brief parsing, approval points, a consent step
  3. StructureDesigned modules with human gates between them

    Brief, journey, copy and visual modules, each producing a draft a person approves or sends back with a comment. A 16-step target design extends this to Risk, Finance and activation.

    the target architecture in my v15 product deck
  4. BuildBuilt it by orchestrating AI agents

    I wrote the campaign skills and prompt specifications the modules run on, and built the software by directing AI coding agents and reviewing their work.

    the skills in my account since June 2026; the development history
  5. Test and iterateTested in the browser and changed what failed

    Automated browser tests ran throughout development. Copy tone was the weakest output, so tone rules are now being refined with Marketing feedback.

    version 15 of the product deck, with each module's status labelled

The evidence items are internal working files. I can walk through them in an interview.

03

How I used AI agents

Agents that built it

  1. An AI coding agent wrote the software from my specifications
  2. Exploration and general-purpose sub-agents worked in parallel sessions
  3. A multi-agent code review checked the codebase
  4. Automated browser tests checked each module as it changed

Agents and rules inside it

  1. Language-model agents draft the journey, the copy and the art direction
  2. A creative-review agent checks visuals against the brief
  3. An image model runs only after human consent
  4. Brief parsing, financial calculations, documents and package QA are rule-based

My part was the product: what each agent is allowed to do, where a rule must replace a model, and where a person decides. I build by directing coding agents and testing the result, not by writing production code by hand.

04

Worked analysis: where should AI help?

Under my working assumptions, most preparation time sits in the four preparation stages, not in Risk and Finance decisions. So those are the stages to assist.

Illustrative assumptions, not measured data

Rough shares based on my own stage assumptions, rounded to the nearest 5%. No stage times have been measured; a pilot would be needed to test them.

Options I compared, and how success would be measured
OptionAssessment
Process fixes onlyCheap, but people still restate the same facts at every step.
Automate end to end, approvals includedRemoves the controls the bank is accountable for.
A generic AI writing toolNo link between brief, scenario and copy, so numbers can drift.
Chosen: assist the four preparation stagesTargets where most time is assumed to go; facts are carried and checked across steps; every decision stays human.

A pilot would compare elapsed days per stage, review rounds before approval, number mismatches caught, and generation cost per campaign, with no change in the time or depth of Risk and Finance review.
