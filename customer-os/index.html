Customer OS

Independent AI-assisted product exploration

An independent project exploring how AI can support customer-focused work and make complex processes easier to manage.

I developed the concept and built a working prototype, drawing on my experience in customer engagement and an interest in practical uses of AI.

01 The idea

Good customer work gets slowed down by repetition.

In my own work I kept seeing the same information rewritten for different people and formats. I wanted to find out whether AI could take on more of that, while people stay in charge of the decisions.

02 My role

Nobody asked for it. I started it, shaped it and built it.

Customer OS is my own initiative, built outside my assigned work.

01Spotted the problem

Noticed it in my own day-to-day work and decided it was worth solving.

02Shaped the product

Decided what it should do, what it should never do, and where people must stay in control.

03Built a working prototype

Built it with AI-assisted development, testing it as I went and changing what didn't work.

03 Product exploration

From a pattern I noticed to something people can try.

The way I approached it, more than what is inside it.

  1. Observe

    A recurring problem in customer-focused work.

  2. Define

    What it should do, and the limits it must respect.

  3. Prototype

    A working version, built with AI-assisted development.

  4. Test

    Tried on test scenarios, not live work.

  5. Refine

    Kept what worked and reworked what didn't.

04 Status

A working prototype. Not a production product.

It runs and I have tested it on test scenarios. It is not used on live work, and I don't claim any measured savings.
