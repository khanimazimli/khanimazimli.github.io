Customer OS

Independent AI-assisted product initiative

I recognised an opportunity to improve customer-related professional work, and independently developed a prototype to explore a practical solution.

I initiated the project, developed the concept, built a working AI-assisted prototype and presented it for evaluation. It was accepted.

The opportunity
Time spent preparing and re-preparing similar information for different people.
My role
Identified the opportunity, initiated the project, defined the concept and scope, built and tested the prototype.
How
AI-assisted development, built and tested by me.
Status
Presented and accepted. A working prototype, initiated and developed independently.
01 The opportunity

The same work, prepared again and again.

In customer-related work, a noticeable share of time goes into preparing similar information in different forms for different colleagues.

I wanted to know whether a focused tool could take some of that work off people's hands, and whether someone in a business role could build a working version of it with today's AI-assisted development tools.

02 Approach

From problem to working prototype.

I treated it as a small product project: a defined problem, a limited scope, a working build, and testing before any claims.

  1. Identify

    Noticed the same preparation work recurring across tasks.

  2. Scope

    Chose which part of the problem to address first and what a useful result would look like.

  3. Build

    Built a working prototype with AI-assisted development.

  4. Test

    Tried it on test scenarios and noted where it fell short.

  5. Iterate

    Reworked the weakest parts and tested again.

03 Status

Presented and accepted.

Customer OS brings together problem identification, product thinking, AI-assisted development and the discipline of presenting an idea for formal evaluation.

Presented and accepted

A working prototype

  • Opportunity identified and project initiated by me
  • Concept and scope defined independently
  • Built with AI-assisted development; runs end to end on test scenarios
  • Tested and revised over several iterations
  • Presented for evaluation and accepted
