Case 01 · Retail banking · Mobile app2026 edition not yet launched

ABB Wrapped 2026: a personal year-in-review, designed to scale across the customer base.

I led the 2026 edition of the bank's year-in-review and redesigned it around reusable story templates that are filled with each customer's values when the story is shown. Backend integration and launch sit with the bank's product and backend teams.

Problem
Turn a year of each customer's payments into a story that is accurate, appropriate and repeatable every December.
My role
Led creative direction, personalization logic, content and production preparation.
Why it was complex
Dozens of story types, many countries, two audiences, and hand-offs across Marketing, Design, product and backend teams.
What I contributed
Story concepts, the template and fallback design, the story set, production tracking and my own pre-handover checks.
Delivered
The 2026 story set prepared and checked for hand-off; product approval, integration and launch are pending. Separately, the 2025 edition, which I contributed to, shipped in December 2025.

01

The scale

Customers' years differ, so the edition is a system of story types, not one story.

  • Dozens of story types across country and travel, spending categories, product firsts, cashback and rewards, and year-summary stories.
  • One master template behind every country story, with a fallback for countries outside the designed set.
  • Separate versions for mass-market and premium customers.
  • Story files tracked and checked before hand-off.

04

Key decisions and constraints

DecisionWhy
Templates instead of designed screensOne master template is designed to serve all country stories and category names of different lengths, so the next edition reuses the system instead of repeating production.
Fallbacks for story familiesA country outside the designed set gets a generic version with its name filled in, to avoid broken or empty screens.
Separate paths for edge segmentsCustomers who left or joined late have no full year to tell, so they get their own sequence instead of a thin summary.
Premium versions for selected stories onlyA distinct premium experience where the audience differs most, without doubling the size of the set.
Test on a slow Android phone before hand-offAnimation problems are invisible on a laptop. Finding them before hand-off is the cheapest point to fix them.
My contribution to the shipped 2025 edition (separate from 2026)

The 2025 edition shipped in December 2025 with more than a dozen story families and over a hundred content variants, covering spending categories, countries, cashback, product firsts, loyalty, and separate paths for churned and new customers.

Tasks assigned to me on the 2025 sprint board: the main story text, the closing message and the messages for churned and new customers; with colleagues, reviewing the category messages and tracking the country and travel visuals. Visual production was done by Design and an agency; Marketing approved the texts.
