ABB Wrapped 2026

A personal year-in-review in the bank's mobile app: a short set of stories about each customer's year with the bank.

I led the 2026 edition: creative direction, content and personalization, working with Marketing, Design, product and backend teams.Approval, integration and launch are owned by the bank's product and backend teams.

Illustrative, with fictional data · not ABB's designs

What it is
An annual, personalized story experience inside a retail bank's app.
My role
Led the 2026 edition, from first concepts to a set ready for hand-off.
Worked with
Marketing, Design, product owners and backend engineers.
Status
Ready for hand-off. Not launched. The 2025 edition, which I contributed to, shipped.
01 The challenge

A year of banking, told as a story people want to share.

Every customer's year is different. The edition has to feel personal to each of them, and it has to be right.

Personal

Stories built around each customer's own year, so it reads like theirs rather than a campaign.

Accurate

Every value a customer sees comes from their own data, and has to be correct.

Coordinated

Creative, content, data and delivery come together across several teams on a fixed date.

02 The experience

Tap through a year in stories.

Illustrative stories with invented people and amounts. Not ABB's designs or the 2026 content.

03 My role

What I led, and what other teams own.

I prepared the edition and checked it before hand-off. Putting it into the app and launching it is with other teams.

Led by me

  • Creative direction and story concepts
  • Content for the story set, developed with AI assistance and reviewed by me
  • The personalization approach: which moments each customer sees
  • Coordination across Marketing, Design, product and backend teams
  • Production tracking and my own checks before hand-off

Owned by other teams
