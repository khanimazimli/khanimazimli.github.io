Case 01 · Retail banking · Mobile app2025 launched2026 in development

ABB Wrapped

A personalized year-in-review in ABB's mobile app. Customers see a summary of their year with the bank, based on their own banking activity.

I contributed customer-facing content to the first edition in 2025. For the expanded 2026 edition, I lead the creative, content and personalization direction and coordinate the teams preparing it.

What it is
An annual, personalized digital experience inside a retail bank's app.
My role
Content contributor in 2025; creative, content and personalization lead for 2026.
Worked with
Marketing, design, product, data and engineering colleagues.
Status
2025 edition launched. The 2026 edition is in development and not yet released.
01 Why customer data matters

Personal only works if it is right.

ABB Wrapped draws on customers' own banking activity, so what a customer sees depends on their year with the bank. That makes it a data product as much as a creative one.

Relevant

The experience should reflect the customer's activity, not a generic message.

Accurate

Details drawn from customer data need to be correct before they reach customers.

Considered

Personal data needs care: deciding what is worth showing, and how, is part of the work.

02 From 2025 to 2026

One initiative, two editions.

The second edition builds on what the first one taught the team.

2025
Launched Dec 2025

First edition

The bank's first personalized year-in-review, released to customers in the app.

My part

Customer-facing story content, written and reviewed with colleagues.

2026
In development

Expanded edition

A larger edition, building on the first. Not yet released.

My part

Creative, content and personalization direction, and coordinating the teams preparing the edition.

03 My responsibilities in 2026

Direction, content and coordination.

I am responsible for what the edition says and how it feels to customers, and for keeping the teams involved working towards the same edition.

  • Creative direction for the edition
  • Developing the content customers will see
  • Personalization direction: which parts of a customer's activity the experience should focus on
  • Coordinating marketing, design, product, data and engineering colleagues
  • Supporting the preparation of the edition for release
Other teams

The data infrastructure, app engineering and the release decision sit with the bank's data, engineering and product teams.

04 What the work involves

A digital product built for a broad and varied customer base.

Working from customer data

Turning banking activity into something a customer recognises as their own.

Product decisions with several teams

Agreeing scope and priorities with colleagues who own different parts.

Quality under a deadline

Balancing creative ambition with accuracy and a fixed release date.

Learning between editions

Carrying what the first edition taught us into the second.
