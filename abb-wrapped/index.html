ABB Wrapped 2026

A personal year-in-review in the bank's mobile app, redesigned for 2026 around reusable story templates that fill with each customer's values when the story is shown.

I led the 2026 edition: creative direction, personalization logic, content and production preparation.Backend integration, approval and launch are owned by the bank's product and backend teams.

Illustrative recreation · fictional customers and values · not ABB's designs

Problem
Turn a year of each customer's payments into a story that is accurate, appropriate and repeatable every December.
Why it was complex
Dozens of story types, many countries, two audiences, and hand-offs across Marketing, Design, product and backend teams.
What I contributed
Story concepts, the template and fallback design, the story set, production tracking and my own pre-handover checks.

01 The challenge

A year of payments is data. A story is a decision about what matters.

Customers' years differ, so the edition cannot be one story. It is a set of story types, each with a rule that decides who sees it and what it says.

04 Key decisions

Three choices that shaped the edition.

Each one took more design work up front, so the edition holds up for different customers and can be reused.

D1

Templates instead of designed screens

Considered
Designing each story screen individually, as a one-off edition.
Chose
One master template per story family, filled with each customer's values when the story is shown.
What it changes
One template is designed to serve all country stories and category names of different lengths, so a future edition can reuse the system rather than repeat production.
D2

Fallbacks instead of gaps

Considered
Designing country stories only for the destinations in the designed set.
Chose
A generic version, filled with the country's name, for any country outside that set.
What it changes
A customer who travelled somewhere unusual still gets a complete story, not a broken or empty screen.
D3

Premium versions only where the audience differs

Considered
A full premium copy of the whole story set.
Chose
Premium versions of selected stories only, such as the opening, the top category and the closing.
What it changes
A distinct premium experience without doubling the size of the set to produce and check.
