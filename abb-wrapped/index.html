ABB Wrapped

Digital Banking · Personalization · Project Ownership

A large-scale personalized banking initiative, built around individual customer activity.

The first edition launched in 2025 and reached approximately 1.5 million customers, delivering personalized year-in-review experiences at scale.

I served as Project Owner for the first edition and continue in the same role for the more advanced 2026 edition, managing cross-functional work and reporting directly to the Managing Director.

What it is
An annual personalized banking experience in ABB's mobile app, built from each customer's own banking activity.
My role
Project Owner for the 2025 and 2026 editions.
Senior reporting
Directly to the Managing Director on this project.
Status
2025 launched to approximately 1.5 million customers. 2026 in development.
01 The initiative

A data-informed product, not a campaign.

Wrapped brings together customer-level banking data, the mobile app and personalization, delivered across several teams to a fixed release date. Its value depends on getting the data, the experience and the delivery right at the same time.

Built on customer data

What a customer sees reflects their own activity with the bank, not a generic message.

Accurate at scale

Personal details have to be right across a very large and varied customer base.

Handled with care

Deciding what is worth showing a customer, and how, is part of the work.

02 Timeline

The 2026 edition is a more advanced continuation of the launched 2025 experience.

Project Owner. Managed the initiative across teams and reported progress directly to the Managing Director.

2026

My role

Project Owner again, managing cross-functional work and reporting directly to the Managing Director.

03 My responsibilities

What project ownership covers.

I own the initiative from direction to results: what each edition should achieve, how the participating teams get there, and what senior management needs to know along the way.

Direction and priorities

Setting what each edition should achieve and which priorities come first.

Experience and personalization

Deciding what the experience shows customers and how personal it should be.

Cross-functional work

Managing the work across marketing, design, product, data and engineering teams.

Delivery

Keeping each edition on track to its release date, with checks before anything reaches customers.

Stakeholder alignment

Agreeing scope and decisions with the colleagues who own each part.

Managing Director reporting

Reporting project progress directly to the Managing Director.

Progress and results

Following delivery and reviewing how the edition performed after launch.

Lessons forward

Using what the first edition showed to shape the second.

Data infrastructure, engineering and technical implementation belong to the bank's data and engineering teams. My role is project ownership, not building the systems.
