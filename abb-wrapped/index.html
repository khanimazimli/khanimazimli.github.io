ABB Wrapped 2026: a personal year-in-review for every customer.

I led the 2026 edition of the bank's year-in-review and rebuilt it from designed screens into a template system that personalizes each story at runtime.

Problem
Turn a year of each customer's payments into a story that is accurate, appropriate and repeatable every December.
My role
Led creative direction, personalization logic, content and production preparation.
Why it was complex
Dozens of story types, 50 countries, two audiences, and hand-offs across Marketing, Design, product and backend teams.
What I contributed
Story concepts, runtime templates and fallbacks, the story set, a production board and a pre-handover QA pass.
Delivered
147 story files built and checked; awaiting product approval and backend integration. The 2025 edition shipped.

04

Key decisions and constraints

DecisionWhy
Templates instead of designed screensOne master template serves 50 countries and every category name length, so the next edition reuses the system instead of repeating production.
A fallback for every story familyA country outside the designed set gets a generic version with its name filled in, so that no customer sees a broken or empty screen.
Separate paths for edge segmentsCustomers who left or joined late have no full year to tell, so they get their own sequence instead of a thin summary.
Premium versions for selected stories onlyA distinct premium experience where the audience differs most, without doubling the size of the set.
Test on a slow Android phone before hand-offAnimation problems are invisible on a laptop. Finding them before hand-off is the cheapest point to fix them.
My work on the shipped 2025 edition

The 2025 edition shipped in December 2025 with 13 story families and 113 content entries: top spending category (19 variants), top country abroad (51), cashback package (10), product firsts (10), loyalty, lottery and brand stories (12), churned and new customers (8), and three shared screens. 110 entries were marked done in production.

I drafted persona definitions for Data Analytics across five lifestyle groups, wrote the main story text, the closing message and the messages for churned and new customers, reviewed the category messages for tone, and tracked redesign and approval of the country and travel visuals. Visual production was done by Design and an agency; Marketing approved the texts.
