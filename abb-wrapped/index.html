01 Personalized banking, at scale

Personal only works if it is right.

Each edition draws on customers' individual banking activity, so the year-in-review a customer receives reflects their own year with the bank. That makes it a data product as much as a creative one.

Relevant

The experience should reflect the customer's activity, not a generic message.

Accurate

Details drawn from customer data need to be correct before they reach customers.

Considered

Personal data needs care: deciding what is worth showing, and how, is part of the work.

03 My role

Project ownership across two editions.

I own the initiative on the business side: what each edition should achieve, how the participating teams work together towards it, and keeping senior management informed.

  • Project Owner for the 2025 and 2026 editions
  • Managing the initiative across marketing, design, product, data and engineering teams
  • Reporting project progress directly to the Managing Director
  • Setting the direction for the experience: content and personalization
  • Preparing the 2026 edition, building on the 2025 launch
Other teams

Data infrastructure, engineering and technical implementation sit with the bank's data and engineering teams. My role is project ownership, not building the systems.

Learning between editions

Carrying what the first edition taught us into the second.
