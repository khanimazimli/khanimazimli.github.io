Customer Journeys

Business analysis, customer experience and initiative delivery

My core work at ABB: analysing customer behaviour and business questions, shaping the customer initiatives I own, and taking them through delivery and review with business and technical teams.

Context
Customer experience and personalization in a retail bank's mobile app.
My role
Lead Customer Experience Specialist. Business owner of my initiatives, from the first question to the review of results.
Worked with
Marketing, data science, risk, finance and engineering colleagues.
Status
Live initiatives that I own, with results reviewed inside the bank.
01 Business context

Which customers need what, and is it worth doing?

A bank's app reaches a large customer base, and many teams compete for that attention. Every initiative has to answer two questions: what a particular group of customers actually needs, and whether offering it is worth the bank's investment.

Answering them well takes three things: evidence about customer behaviour, a clear business goal, and a review afterwards of whether the initiative did what it was meant to.

A Data and customer analysis

Checking the assumption before the decision.

I work with customer information to understand behaviour, investigate business questions and support decisions. For straightforward retrieval and checks, I use basic SQL myself. Modelling and deeper data work sit with the data science team.

  1. 01
    Frame

    Turn a business question into something the data can answer.

  2. 02
    Examine

    Retrieve and check the relevant information, with basic SQL where needed.

  3. 03
    Interpret

    Explain what the patterns show and what they mean for the initiative.

B Business decisions

Shaping initiatives the bank can justify.

Analysis matters only when it changes what the bank does. My part is connecting what customers need with what the business requires, and proposing initiatives that serve both.

Understanding requirements

What the business needs an initiative to achieve, and the constraints it has to respect.

Assessing customer needs

Which customers the initiative is for, and what would actually be useful to them.

Shaping the initiative

Defining the proposal: its audience, its goal and how success will be judged.

Evaluating the evidence

Weighing the available information before committing the bank's effort.

Supporting priorities

Helping decide what goes first, and what should wait or not happen at all.

C Ownership and delivery

The business owner, in a team of specialists.

I own the business side of my initiatives. That means managing the requirements within my scope, coordinating the business and technical teams who build and approve them, following execution, and assessing the results once they are live.

The models, the build, and the risk and finance decisions belong to the colleagues who own them. The initiatives I own are live in the app, and I review their results with the team. Performance figures are internal.
