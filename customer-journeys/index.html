Customer Journeys

Customer analysis, business decisions and delivery

My day-to-day work at ABB: understanding what customers do and need, deciding what the bank should offer them and when, and seeing those initiatives through to results with the teams involved.

Context
Customer experience and personalization in a retail bank's mobile app.
My role
Lead Customer Experience Specialist. I own the business side of my initiatives.
Worked with
Marketing, data science, risk, finance and engineering colleagues.
Status
Live initiatives that I own, with results reviewed inside the bank.
01 Business context

Reach is easy. Relevance takes work.

A bank's app can reach a large customer base, and many teams want that attention. The useful question is narrower: what does a particular group of customers actually need, and is it worth the bank's investment to offer it?

Answering that well means understanding customers through their behaviour, being clear about the business goal, and checking afterwards whether the initiative did what it was meant to.

02 My contribution

Three parts of the same job.

For the initiatives I own, I am responsible for the business side from the first question to the review of results.

A

Customer and data analysis

I work with customer information, look at behavioural patterns and investigate business questions. When I need to, I use basic SQL to retrieve and examine the relevant data myself.

B

Business decisions

I connect what the analysis shows with customer experience and campaign initiatives: who they are for, what they should achieve, and whether they are worth doing.

C

Project delivery

I work with business and technical colleagues to get initiatives built and launched, and I review how they perform so the next one is better informed.

03 Data and analysis

Some questions need more than a meeting or a brief.

Before a decision is made, I check the assumption behind it against customer data. When I need to look more closely, I use basic SQL to pull and review the information myself, then bring the finding back to the team.

  1. 01
    Ask

    Turn a business question into something the data can answer.

  2. 02
    Look

    Retrieve and check the relevant information, with basic SQL where needed.

  3. 03

    Explain what the data shows and what it means for the initiative.

04 Working across teams

The business side, in a team of specialists.

In a regulated bank, an initiative needs several specialists. My part is the business side: framing the need, agreeing what the initiative should achieve, and keeping it moving to launch. The models, the build and the risk and finance decisions belong to the colleagues who own them.

05 Delivery and outcomes

Live, reviewed, and improved.

Initiatives I own are live in the app. I review their results with the team and carry what we learn into the next one. Performance figures are internal and are not published here.

Start from the customer question

Decide what the customer needs before deciding which channel or format to use.

Check before deciding

A quick look at the data often changes the question, and saves effort later.

A good plan can be delivered

The best initiative is one the team can build, launch and measure.
