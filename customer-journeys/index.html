Case 02 · Retail banking · Mobile appCampaigns live

Who.When.Whether.

Customer Journeys. My day-to-day work is deciding who a campaign is for, when it should reach them, and when the bank should not send anything at all.

The problem
Many teams want the same customers' attention. Sending everything is noise, not personalization.
My role
Lead Customer Experience Specialist. I own my campaigns on the business side.
Worked with
Business squads, Data Science, Risk, Finance and the team that builds and launches campaigns.
Status
Live campaigns I own, with more in approval or development.
01 The business problem

More messages do not mean better personalization.

Every campaign can be approved on its own and still add up to noise for the customer. The decision that matters is made for each customer, not for each campaign.

Broadcast

Every campaign goes to everyone

Each customer receives several messages, whether or not they fit.

Decided

Each customer gets what fits, or nothing

Some receive one relevant message. Others are deliberately left alone.

Conceptual illustration with fictional customers.

02 How I use data

Some questions need more than a meeting or a campaign brief.

I look at customer data to check an assumption or explain a pattern before a decision is made. When I need to examine the information more closely, I use basic SQL to pull and review it myself.

  1. 01
    Ask

    Turn a business question into something the data can answer.

  2. 02
    Look

    Pull and check the relevant information, with basic SQL where needed.

  3. 03
    Decide

    Bring what I found back to the team, with what it means for the campaign.

Abstract illustration. No real data.
03 My responsibility

What I decide, and what other teams own.

For the campaigns I own, I am responsible for the business side, from the first insight to the results.

My part

  • Customer insight: who a campaign is for, and why
  • Personalization and timing for my campaigns
  • The offer and message sequence, in three languages
  • Taking each campaign through its approvals
  • Hand-off, launch, and reviewing the results with the team

Owned by other teams

  • Scoring models, by Data Science
  • Building and configuring campaigns, by the engagement team
  • Cost and risk approval, by Risk and Finance
04 Execution and learning

Live campaigns, and what they taught me.

My live campaigns range from task-and-reward offers to a premium birthday reward and an in-app feedback survey. Results are reported inside the bank and are not published here.

Relevance beats reach

A smaller audience that fits the offer does more than a large one that doesn't.

Holding back is a decision

Not sending a message is often the right call, even when every campaign is approved.

Write it so others can act

A campaign is ready when the people who build and approve it can work from it without me in the room.
