01 The problem

One customer.Seven messages.

This customer qualifies for seven different journeys at the same time.

Every one of them is technically relevant. But sending all seven would turn personalization into noise.

02 How the decision is made

Before we send anything,we ask four questions.

01🎯

Does it matter to this customer?

Does the customer's current situation make this journey relevant?

02⏰

Is this the right moment?

A relevant message at the wrong time is still the wrong message.

03🗣️

Have we already spoken to them?

Avoid repeated or competing contact.

04⭐

Is something more important?

When journeys overlap, the experience with the strongest customer need comes first.

Every message ends up with one of three answers.

SendThis is what matters now.Priority · arbitration
WaitRelevant, but not yet.Timing · frequency
StopNo longer right for this customer.Suppression

04 The quiet decision

Sometimesthe best messageis no message.

If the customer was contacted yesterday, already did what we were going to ask, or has something more important going on, the journey waits.

That is also personalization.

Relevance also means knowing when to stay quiet.
