At ABB, a retail bank in Azerbaijan, I moved from customer experience into the hyper-personalization squad, where I now work as Lead CX Specialist on personalized campaigns and customer journeys in the mobile app. Alongside that, I build AI-assisted tools to take repetitive steps out of campaign work.
